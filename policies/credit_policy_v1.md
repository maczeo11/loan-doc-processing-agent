# Retail Credit Underwriting Policy (v1.0)

**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01  
**Classification:** Internal Authoritative Policy

---

## 1. Income & Employment Eligibility

1.1 **Minimum Salary Threshold**  
The applicant must have a documented gross monthly salary of at least ₹25,000 (INR) or net monthly salary of ₹20,000 (INR).

1.2 **Employment Tenure**  
The applicant must demonstrate continuous employment with the current employer for a minimum of 6 months, verified via payslips and bank payroll credits.

1.3 **Salary Credit Verification**  
Payslip net salary must reconcile with corresponding bank statement credits within a 5.0% tolerance margin. Discrepancies exceeding 5.0% require underwriter manual flag and secondary review.

---

## 2. Debt-to-Income (DTI) & Obligation Limits

2.1 **Maximum Debt-to-Income (DTI)**  
The total monthly debt obligations (including the proposed loan installment) must not exceed 50% of the applicant's verified net monthly income.

2.2 **Average Monthly Balance (AMB)**  
The applicant's primary bank account must maintain an average monthly balance of at least ₹5,000 across the trailing 3-month statement window.

---

## 3. Mandatory Documentation Checklist

Every retail loan application dossier must include:
1. Loan Application Form with signed declaration.
2. Recent 3 consecutive months' Salary Payslips.
3. 3 to 6 months' Bank Account Statement showing regular salary credits.
4. Latest Income Tax Return (ITR-V Acknowledgement) or Form 16.
5. Government-issued KYC Proof (PAN Card and Aadhaar/Passport).
